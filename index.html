A major celebration week, not a checklist holiday. Exotic like Thailand without feeling seedy. Not a cliché Caribbean package. Not 36 hours to Bora Bora.

Seychelles is granite islands in the Indian Ocean: jungle meeting white sand, water that looks unreal, short mountain walks that end at a beach. About 6 hours 20 minutes from Tel Aviv. Base: Mahé. Optional second island: Praslin, fifteen minutes on a land plane. No ferries.

Where it is

Western Indian Ocean, about 1,600 km east of Kenya, northeast of Madagascar. Not the Caribbean. Not the Pacific.

The rules we already set

  • No boats. No ferries. No sunset catamaran. No La Digue unless we later pay for a helicopter.
  • Praslin is allowed — only on the Air Seychelles Twin Otter (runway, 15 minutes).
  • We cook. Need oven + stovetop + full fridge. Four Seasons one-bedroom treehouses are out — minibar only.
  • Small supermarkets exist. Best shop on Mahé: SPAR Eden Island. On Praslin: ISPC Baie Sainte Anne.
  • No Chabad House on either island. Pack staples from home.
  • Car: yes, on whichever island we sleep. Drive on the left. Compact automatic. Do not move one car between islands.
  • Rhythm: easy hike in the morning, beach and sun after.
  • Beaches close to the house — walk or a few minutes, not a 40-minute hunt every afternoon.

The plane hop (not a boat)

There are no Maldives-style seaplanes. Air Seychelles flies a Twin Otter from Mahé’s domestic terminal (two-minute walk from international) to Praslin Airport (PRI). Air time 15–20 minutes. Many flights a day. About €80–150 one way per person, €160–280 return. Baggage typically 23 kg + 5 kg. Check-in 45–60 minutes.

That is the only way we add Praslin. La Digue has no runway.

Sample 10 days on Mahé

Works from any of the Mahé houses. If we add Praslin, swap the last four days for the Praslin block below.

  1. Land SEZ, SPAR Eden Island shop, drive to the house, swim or pool, early night.
  2. Recover on the nearest beach. Learn the coastal road only.
  3. Anse Major hike (starts near Bel Ombre): walk to a hidden beach, picnic, swim, walk back.
  4. Copolia morning (granite viewpoint), beach after.
  5. Morne Blanc early, then Port Launay or Beau Vallon if home beach is sloppy.
  6. Restock + villa day.
  7. Anse Intendance / Baie Lazare for pictures. Swim only if that cove is calm.
  8. Victoria market + Mission Lodge or Jardin du Roi + beach.
  9. Repeat the favorite hike and a long swim.
  10. No hike. Airport 30–45 minutes. Slack.

Praslin — the second island

Smaller and slower than Mahé. About 11 km long. Same granite-and-palm look, plus two things Mahé does not have: Anse Lazio (the beach people mean when they say Seychelles) and Vallée de Mai (UNESCO coco-de-mer forest).

We get there on the Twin Otter. We rent a second small car at PRI. We cook. Same rhythm: morning walk, afternoon beach.

Water ~27–28°C. Dry-ish month. Anse Lazio is not closed in June. It is better early. Côte d’Or is the afternoon backup.

Morning adventures (all on land)

  • Vallée de Mai — UNESCO palm forest. Easy shaded loops, 1.5–2 hours. Go at opening. Ticket around SCR 350–450 (€25–30).
  • Fond Ferdinand — same coco-de-mer idea, fewer people, a real viewpoint over Praslin / La Digue / Curieuse. ~1.5–2 hours.
  • Anse Lazio → Anse Georgette — coastal trail, ~3 km / ~2 hours. Medium. Swim at both ends if the water is behaving.
  • Glacis Noire — short interior viewpoint near Vallée de Mai.

Praslin is not Mahé for ridge drama. Forest + beach-to-beach, not Morne Blanc.

If we add Praslin after Mahé

One move. Drop the Mahé car, Twin Otter, pick up a Praslin car, shop ISPC, house.

  1. Anse Lazio early, swim, do nothing.
  2. Vallée de Mai at opening, Côte d’Or after.
  3. Fond Ferdinand or Lazio→Georgette hike, beach.
  4. Buffer morning, hop back to SEZ, fly home.

Shapes, not a decision: Mahé only (10) · Praslin only (10, hop on arrival) · Mahé then Praslin (6+4 or 5+5) · Praslin then Mahé (finish near the international airport).

Cost of adding Praslin: two extra one-way tickets (~€160–300 for two), a second car (~€40–70/day), one extra shop. House rates sit in the same band as Mahé kitchens.

Money picture (10 nights, two people)

  • House: €1,400–4,000 for the couple-sized options (or €5,000–7,000 for a fancy Beau Vallon villa)
  • Car: €500–800 (plus a second car if we split islands)
  • Food: €200–400
  • Praslin hop, if we take it: ~€160–280 return for two
  • Flights TLV–SEZ: often ~$800–1,500 per person depending on routing

Total ballpark excluding international flights: about €2,200–5,200 for the house+car+food we actually want.
