Where it is

Western Indian Ocean, east of Kenya, northeast of Madagascar. One hour ahead of Israel in June.

Airport and Victoria on the east. Granite coves south-west. Beau Vallon north-west. June: north and west swim better. Anse Soleil usually stays usable.

Airbnb and other kitchens — not hotels

Yes. On Mahé the good self-catering places are villas and apartments. Better fit than a resort: oven, fridge, walk to a cove.

Filter: Entire place · Kitchen · then message: “oven + stovetop + full fridge? walking minutes to a June swim beach?”
