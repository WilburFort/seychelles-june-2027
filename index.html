A major celebration week, not a checklist holiday. Exotic like Thailand without feeling seedy. Not a cliché Caribbean package. Not 36 hours to Bora Bora.

Seychelles is granite islands in the Indian Ocean: jungle meeting white sand, water that looks unreal, short mountain walks that end at a beach. About 6 hours 20 minutes from Tel Aviv. We stay on Mahé only. No ferries. No island-hopping.

Where it is

Western Indian Ocean, about 1,600 km east of Kenya, northeast of Madagascar. Not the Caribbean. Not the Pacific.

In June the southeast trade winds make south-facing wild beaches choppier. North and west swim more reliably. Anse Soleil is a small west-facing cove that usually stays usable.

The rules we already set

  • Mahé only. No Praslin, no La Digue, no sunset catamaran.
  • We cook. Need oven + stovetop + full fridge. Four Seasons one-bedroom treehouses are out — minibar only.
  • Small supermarkets exist. Best shop: SPAR Eden Island near Victoria.
  • No Chabad House on the islands. Pack staples from home.
  • Car: yes. Drive on the left. Compact automatic from the airport.
  • Rhythm: easy hike in the morning, beach and sun after.
  • Beaches close to the house — walk or a few minutes, not a 40-minute hunt every afternoon.

Seaplanes / private pilot

There are no Maldives-style seaplanes. Air Seychelles flies a 15-minute Twin Otter Mahé–Praslin (runway, not water). Zil Air helicopters cost ~€900+ per aircraft. We do not need either if we stay on Mahé. Optional treat later: a 20-minute scenic helicopter over Mahé so we see the other islands from the air and land back on the same island.

Airbnb and other homes — not hotels

Yes. On Mahé the magical spots are mostly private villas and self-catering houses, not resorts. Airbnb exists. The local specialist SeyVillas is often cleaner to book. Booking.com “homes / villas” and VRBO show many of the same doors.

Filter every listing for: entire place, kitchen with oven + stovetop + full fridge (not a kitchenette / minibar), 1 bedroom is enough, walk-to-beach or under 10 minutes by car.

West — Port Glaud

Better June water at Port Launay. Closer to the mountain hikes.

  • Villas de Jardin — also on Airbnb as a 2BR tropical retreat / Maison de Jardin. Kitchen. Shared pool.
  • Gigi’s Self-Catering Apt, Port Glaud — 2 bedroom, kitchen, long Airbnb history. search Port Glaud
  • Island of Love / hillside self-catering, Port Glaud — check Booking holiday homes.

Ask: oven, not only a two-burner hob. Port Launay beach is a few minutes by car, not always a walk.

North — Beau Vallon / Bel Ombre

Town beach, but the water works in June. ISPC is on the beach road. Anse Major trail starts at Bel Ombre.

  • Chez Nella — house a few minutes from Beau Vallon, stove + fridge (confirm oven). On Airbnb.
  • Mouggae Blues Villas — ~100 m from Beau Vallon beach. SeyVillas
  • Villa Jasmin, Bel Ombre — near the Anse Major trailhead. SeyVillas
  • Search Airbnb for “entire place + kitchen + Beau Vallon” and ignore anything that only lists a microwave.

Four Seasons Residences at Petite Anse (not the standard treehouse rooms) are the rare resort option with a full kitchen. Beautiful and expensive. Only worth it if the Residence calendar is open and the price is not silly for 10 nights.

Sample 10 days

Works from any of the houses.

  1. Land SEZ, SPAR Eden Island shop, drive to the house, swim or pool, early night.
  2. Recover on the nearest beach. Learn the coastal road only.
  3. Anse Major hike (starts near Bel Ombre): walk to a hidden beach, picnic, swim, walk back. No boat.
  4. Copolia morning (granite viewpoint), beach after.
  5. Morne Blanc early, then Port Launay or Beau Vallon if home beach is sloppy.
  6. Restock + villa day.
  7. Anse Intendance / Baie Lazare for pictures. Swim only if that cove is calm.
  8. Victoria market + Mission Lodge or Jardin du Roi + beach.
  9. Repeat the favorite hike and a long swim.
  10. No hike. Airport 30–45 minutes. Slack.
