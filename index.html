A trip concept for the two of us

Seychelles, June 2027

Fifty. Thirty years married. One island. No boats. A kitchen. Hills in the morning, water in the afternoon.

The idea

Mahé only. About 10 nights after Shavuot (leave after 12 June 2027, or before 10 June). A house with oven, stove and a full fridge. Beach close enough to walk. No ferries, no second hotel.

  • Mahé only. No boats. No seaplanes needed.
  • We cook. No Chabad, no kosher restaurant. SPAR Eden Island on day one.
  • Real kitchen: oven, stove, full fridge.
  • Car from the airport. Drive on the left.
  • Four Seasons one-bedroom treehouses are out — no kitchen.

Four houses

Ten days

  1. Land. SPAR shop. House. Early night.
  2. Recover on the sand.
  3. Anse Major hike to a hidden beach. No boat.
  4. Copolia granite viewpoint. Swim after.
  5. Morne Blanc at sunrise. Swim after.
  6. Restock. Do nothing.
  7. Anse Intendance for pictures. Swim only if calm.
  8. Victoria market. A viewpoint. Beach.
  9. Repeat the favourite morning.
  10. Buffer. Fly.
