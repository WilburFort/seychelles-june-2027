For the two of us

June 2027 — Seychelles

30th wedding anniversary & 50th birthdays. One island. No boats. A kitchen. Morning hikes. Afternoon beach.

A major celebration week, not a checklist holiday. Exotic like Thailand without feeling seedy. Not a cliché Caribbean package. Not 36 hours to Bora Bora.

Seychelles is granite islands in the Indian Ocean: jungle meeting white sand, water that looks unreal, short mountain walks that end at a beach. About 6 hours 20 minutes from Tel Aviv. We stay on Mahé only. No ferries. No island-hopping.

Where it is

Western Indian Ocean, about 1,600 km east of Kenya, northeast of Madagascar. Not the Caribbean. Not the Pacific.

Mahé is the main island (~27 km long). Airport (SEZ) and Victoria are on the east/northeast. Mountains down the middle. Beaches all around. In June the southeast trade winds make south-facing wild beaches choppier. North and west swim more reliably. Anse Soleil is a small west-facing cove that usually stays usable.

The rules we already set

  • Mahé only. No Praslin, no La Digue, no sunset catamaran.
  • We cook. Need oven + stovetop + full fridge.
  • Small supermarkets exist. Best shop: SPAR Eden Island near Victoria.
  • No Chabad House on the islands. Pack staples from home.
  • Car: yes. Drive on the left. Compact automatic from the airport.
  • Rhythm: easy hike in the morning, beach and sun after.
  • Beaches close to the house — walk or a few minutes, not a 40-minute hunt every afternoon.

Seaplanes / private pilot

There are no Maldives-style seaplanes. Air Seychelles flies a 15-minute Twin Otter Mahé–Praslin (runway, not water). Zil Air helicopters cost ~€900+ per aircraft. We do not need either if we stay on Mahé. Optional treat later: a 20-minute scenic helicopter over Mahé so we see the other islands from the air and land back on the same island.

Sample 10 days

Works from any of the four houses.

  1. Land SEZ, SPAR Eden Island shop, drive to the house, swim or pool, early night.
  2. Recover on the nearest beach. Learn the coastal road only.
  3. Anse Major hike (starts near Bel Ombre): walk to a hidden beach, picnic, swim, walk back. No boat.
  4. Copolia morning (granite viewpoint), beach after.
  5. Morne Blanc early, then Port Launay or Beau Vallon if home beach is sloppy.
  6. Restock + villa day.
  7. Anse Intendance / Baie Lazare for pictures. Swim only if that cove is calm.
  8. Victoria market + Mission Lodge or Jardin du Roi + beach.
  9. Repeat the favorite hike and a long swim.
  10. No hike. Airport 30–45 minutes. Slack.

Money picture (10 nights, two people)

  • House: €1,400–4,000 for the couple-sized options (or €5,000–7,000 for a fancy Beau Vallon villa)
  • Car: €500–800
  • Food: €200–400
  • Flights TLV–SEZ: often ~$800–1,500 per person depending on routing (Air Seychelles has operated a ~6h20 weekly)

Total ballpark excluding flights: about €2,200–5,200 for the house+car+food we actually want.
